Sales Dashboard

Interactive Power BI Sales Dashboard with 4 sections: Executive Summary, Profitability, Geospatial Analysis, and Notes.

Executive Sales Dashboard

Purpose: Provides leadership with a high-level view of overall performance (Revenue, Profit, Orders, Return Rates).
Insights: Highlights trends vs targets and areas requiring deeper review.

Profitability Analysis

Purpose: Tracks margins and cost structures across products and categories.
Insights: Identifies profit drivers, cost leakages, and underperforming segments.

Geospatial Revenue Analysis

Purpose: Provides visibility of revenue distribution by geography.
Insights: Identifies strong regions, underperforming areas, and growth opportunities.

Notes

Purpose: Serves as a commentary and documentation section for management.
Insights: Consolidates key findings, action points, and follow-ups.
